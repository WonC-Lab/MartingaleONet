# Preregistered hypothesis candidates and refutation rules

Status: design thresholds, not observed outcomes. Freeze these thresholds/populations after E01 feasibility and a development-only pilot, before any final-test access. Any change must be versioned and justified without final results. Failure/inconclusive outcomes are valid; reference uncertainty must not be used to excuse unfavorable rankings.

## Common metric definitions

Price error e_c=(Vhat-Vref)/K, nRMSE=sqrt(mean(e_c²)),nMAE,median absolute,p95,p99,worst; relative L² and price relative errors only for Vref/K>=1e-5. No nearzero MAPE headline. Delta error physical units; Gamma error K*(Gamma_hat-Gamma_ref); variance error (nu_hat-nu_ref)/K. Report RMSE,p95,worst for each plus regime slices. Train-derived fixed scales can be used for aggregate optimization metrics, never hide physical values.

Constraint shortfall g: price lower/upper dividedK; spot slope negative/above1; negativeKGamma; strike derivative outside[-exp(-rtau),0]; negativeK*V_KK; calendar slope sign in the declared r>=0,q0 regime. Gamma/strike-convexity are not independent evidence under homogeneity. Violation frequency is mean1(g>eta),mean magnitude all points,mean among violations,p95,worst and worst coordinates. Thresholds: eta_bound=1e-6 normalized price,eta_slope=1e-5,eta_curvature=1e-4 normalized curvature; reference certification tolerances are disclosed alongside these operational thresholds. Analytic necessary signs use zero; numerical empirical violations use these explicit thresholds and a tolerance sensitivity plot. All tolerances are design budgets, not predictions of achieved performance.

Terminal short-call PnL Pi_j uses common reference premium, identical path/state and settlement. Loss=-Pi; VaR95=quantile_.95(loss),ES95=mean loss among worst5% (document finite-sample interpolation); report signed PnL mean separately. std uses sample ddof1,variance=std²,RMSE=sqrt(mean(Pi²)); price/Greek per-row error stats use mean over the declared population, no implicit Bessel convention. Turnover=sum S_t|delta change|,paidTC=alpha*turnover; closing convention fixed in E08/E09.

## Evaluation and inference

Final interpolation/boundary/OOD populations are those in [research design](PHASE1A_RESEARCH_DESIGN.md); an IID certification stream independent of training/model selection is additional if population-probability certificates are attempted. No final test is used to choose weights,capacity,checkpoint or model. Development-selected checkpoints are frozen before evaluation. Compare both a declared fixed-budget checkpoint and a price-matched development-selected checkpoint; the latter is chosen without final Greek information.

Use five paired training seeds 5101–5105 for development/exploration. Decide whether to fund the five additional seeds 5106–5110 using development evidence and compute budget only, before final-test access. If confirming hypotheses, freeze all ten seeds and configurations before evaluating any final test; do not look at the first five final outcomes and then decide to add five more. Share corresponding data/sampler seeds and initial weights between same-architecture objective arms; other architectures receive deterministic independent initializations keyed by seed. The 65,536 test rows are not independent training replications. Use 10,000 hierarchical bootstrap resamples over seed and parameter blocks (hedges: contract/regime and common path blocks), reporting individual seeds and small-seed limitations. Five-seed final reports are exploratory only.

Confirmatory directional median effects for H2/H4/H5 use exact paired seed sign tests (ties counted conservatively), Holm adjustment across the3hypotheses at family alpha.05. These test median seed-direction, not effect-size magnitude; require the declared practical effect as well and a positive/negative appropriate paired CI. H3 is a predeclared noninferiority condition; confidence upper bound is reported with its assumptions and bootstrap sensitivity rather than treated as a large-sample theorem with10seeds. If10seeds are unaffordable, label results exploratory and do not claim statistical confirmation. H1 is an existence/failure hypothesis, H6 a benchmark/economics hypothesis, analyzed separately. Secondary metrics and subgroups are exploratory unless explicitly preregistered; no hunting for one favorable regime.

## Additional mechanism questions

M1: same homogeneity means spot andstrike curvature diagnostics agree exactly after scaling; discrepancies signal evaluation bugs. M2: path-weightedDelta risk predicts common-path stock-hedge gain deviation better than price nRMSE (Spearman correlation and held-out regression, regime-block split); association does not prove causation. M3: shape scores add predictive information beyond Greek errors; use a predeclared nested-regression comparison on development models and frozen holdout checkpoints. M4: lower GammaTV is not necessarily better—reference Gamma structure matters. These mechanism analyses may supply an informative negative result if the proposed model does not dominate.
